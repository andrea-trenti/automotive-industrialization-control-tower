# Online Research Basis

Methodological basis. The framework uses public methodological guidance rather than proprietary checklists. AIAG APQP 3rd Edition emphasizes sourcing, change management, program metrics, risk mitigation and gated management. AACE schedule-risk guidance supports Monte Carlo analysis tied to CPM schedules and integrated cost/schedule risk analysis. NASA's Schedule Management Handbook provides schedule-management best practices and EVM integrates scope, schedule and cost. These references inform terminology and credibility boundaries; all numerical program data in this repository are synthetic.

Sources: AIAG APQP-3 (2024); AACE 117R-21, 132R-23 and schedule-risk guidance; NASA Schedule Management Handbook (2024); PMI scheduling/PERT/CPM references; NIST VVUQ concepts.
